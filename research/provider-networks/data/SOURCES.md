# Reference data

Small public inputs, committed so that the study runs from a clone plus a graph directory. The notebook reads them with `read_csv`. Shared geography (ZIP code to HRR, and ZIP code to county to CBSA) is in [`reference/`](../../../reference/SOURCES.md), with its sources and the rule for mapping a ZIP code to a county.

| File | What | Source |
|---|---|---|
| `hospital_enrollments_2026-09-29.csv` | CMS's crosswalk from each hospital, critical access hospital and rural emergency hospital Medicare enrollment to its CCN, of the same date as the graph's last PPEF quarter: `enrollment_id`, `provider_type_code`, `npi`, `ccn`, `cah_or_hospital_ccn`, `pac_id`. 9,138 enrollments. Used only to measure the precision and recall of the graph's hospital link. | CMS, *Hospital Enrollments*, data.cms.gov, file `Hospital_Enrollments_2026.09.29.csv` (https://data.cms.gov/sites/default/files/2026-10/961708ac-2136-43cb-9587-ac64af23b6ab/Hospital_Enrollments_2026.09.29.csv, sha256 `5f02975f…e9e281a5`). Columns `ENROLLMENT ID`, `PROVIDER TYPE CODE`, `NPI`, `CCN`, `CAH OR HOSPITAL CCN` and `ASSOCIATE ID` kept and renamed; names, addresses and the other 33 columns dropped. |

| `snf_enrollments_2026-09-29.csv`, `hha_enrollments_2026-09-29.csv` | CMS's crosswalks from each skilled nursing facility (14,404) and home health agency (11,522) Medicare enrollment to its CCN, of the graph's last PPEF date, with the same five columns as the hospital file. Used only to score the facility link. | CMS, *Skilled Nursing Facility Enrollments* and *Home Health Agency Enrollments*, data.cms.gov: `SNF_Enrollments_2026.09.29.csv` (https://data.cms.gov/sites/default/files/2026-10/4674b04e-948d-45c6-b70e-2ad30b3e7b0d/SNF_Enrollments_2026.09.29.csv, sha256 `7cbe1537a70ce836…`) and `HHA_Enrollments_2026.09.29.csv` (https://data.cms.gov/sites/default/files/2026-10/0089fc1e-f43a-4c6d-b50c-8f1a208c1c7c/HHA_Enrollments_2026.09.29.csv, sha256 `d76ecbb2b50a7047…`). | `owner_audit_pairs.csv` | 250 pairs of organizations that share an NPPES key on 30 December 2022, 50 for each kind (`ao` official, `mail` address, `phone`, `parent` subpart parent, `staff` Compendium groups in staffing families), drawn at random: both associate ids, legal names and states, and a hand label (`same` owner 98, `different` 25, `unclear` 127). A third of the enrollments in the CMS file carry a CCN that is not a POS hospital: swing-bed (`xxZxxx`), psychiatric-unit (`xxSxxx`), rehabilitation-unit (`xxTxxx`) and similar CCNs that share the hospital's address and NPI. `cah_or_hospital_ccn` names the parent hospital for some of them, and the notebook checks the link only on the enrollments whose CCN, or parent CCN, is an active POS hospital.

## Not committed

The owner communities' other evidence reaches the notebook through the warehouse: `healthcare-ledger download --source-name irs` (the Nonprofit Open Data Collective's parquet tables of the e-filed Form 990s, tax years 2013-2025, and the IRS EO BMF; `releases/irs_sources.csv`), `hcris` (CMS's cost-report zips; `releases/hcris_sources.csv`), `chow` (CMS's hospital and SNF change-of-ownership files of 29 September 2026; `releases/chow_sources.csv`) and `nhc` (Internet Archive captures of Nursing Home Compare's monthly `Ownership_Download.csv`, one per quarter 2014-2022; `releases/nhc_sources.csv`), each `load`ed into its own DuckDB file (docs/METHODOLOGY.md section 7d, docs/MAINTAINING.md). The EIN-to-NPI rosters are the Transparency in Coverage heads of `research/tic-spike/` (`data/tic/heads`, October 2026).

The AHRQ Compendium of U.S. Health Systems files (section 6) reach the notebook through the graph: `healthcare-ledger download --source-name ahrq` fetches the files `releases/ahrq_sources.csv` pins (about 150 MB, sha256-checked), `load --source-name ahrq` puts them in the warehouse, and `graph-build --compendium` turns them into `system` and `system_link` (docs/METHODOLOGY.md section 8a). Source: Agency for Healthcare Research and Quality, *Compendium of U.S. Health Systems*, editions 2016 (updated 2020), 2018 (updated 2021) and 2020 to 2023, https://www.ahrq.gov/chsp/data-resources/compendium.html; files under https://www.ahrq.gov/sites/default/files/wysiwyg/chsp/compendium/, with the Internet Archive's captures of the same URLs where ahrq.gov refuses scripts. The group practice linkage files are by Mathematica for AHRQ (e.g. *Compendium of U.S. Health Systems, 2022, Group Practice Linkage File, Technical Documentation*, AHRQ Publication No. 25-0011-1-EF, December 2024).


HHS's Medicaid spending extract (section 4b) reaches the notebook through the warehouse and `graph-build --medicaid`, which writes `medicaid_volume` and `main_group` from it: `healthcare-ledger download --source-name medicaid` fetches the zip `releases/medicaid_sources.csv` pins (`medicaid-provider-spending.csv.zip`, 3,761,921,469 bytes, sha256 `3387c48ea9600c15…`), and `load --source-name medicaid` puts its CSV (12 GB unzipped) in `data/warehouse/medicaid.duckdb` as the table `medicaid_spending`, every column kept. Source: HHS, *Medicaid Provider Spending*, release of 2026-02-09, https://opendata.hhs.gov/datasets/medicaid-provider-spending; file https://stopendataprod.blob.core.windows.net/datasets/medicaid-provider-spending/2026-02-09/dataset/medicaid-provider-spending.csv.zip. It is the public provider-level extract of T-MSIS outpatient and professional claims. One row per billing NPI, servicing NPI, HCPCS code and month, 2018 to 2024, with patients, claim lines and Medicaid paid; cells with fewer than 12 patients or claim lines are suppressed, and there is no state column. `graph-build` reads `billing_npi`, `servicing_npi`, `month` and `paid` (HHS's `BILLING_PROVIDER_NPI_NUM`, `SERVICING_PROVIDER_NPI_NUM`, `CLAIM_FROM_MONTH` and `TOTAL_PAID`). The same release also offers the file as `.parquet`, `.duckdb` and `.vortex`, and a sibling drug-level file by NDC (`medicaid-provider-spending-ndc.csv.zip`); neither is used.
